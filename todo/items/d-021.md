# d-021: Place names in the page titles; "Londra" and "ehliyet" on the Turkish pages

- Status: done
- Priority: P1
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

- English home page: put the areas into the `<title>` and H1. At the moment only the meta
  description names a place.
- Turkish pages: use the words Turkish speakers search with, "Londra" and "ehliyet".

## Why

The title is the strongest signal of what a page is about. Turkish searches for an instructor in
London found nothing from London on 2026-11-04, so there is almost no competition there.

## Done when

The new titles are live, with the wording agreed with the owner.
