## What

Step 7 of the 2026-11-19 search and AI visibility pass. Add questions to the home page FAQ (EN and TR)
the way people now ask ChatGPT or Google, each answered in its first sentence, in Seyma's words, and
mark the FAQ up as `FAQPage` in the JSON-LD. Candidate questions (Seyma decides which are true for her):

- Do you teach nervous drivers?

- Which test centre near Chingford is easiest? (DVSA pass rates in d-023; no promises)

## Why

AI assistants quote short, direct answers to the exact question asked. Google no longer shows FAQ rich
results for most sites, so the markup is cheap help for machines, not a ranking trick.
