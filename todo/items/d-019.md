# d-019: Bing Places and Apple Business Connect

- Status: open (owner, later)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

- Bing Places: add the listing (bing.com/forbusiness), verify it by PIN, hide the address.
- Apple Business Connect: claim or add the business, so it shows in Apple Maps and Siri.

Use the same name, phone, website and areas as the Google profile.

## Why

They cover the people who don't search on Google: iPhone users in Apple Maps, and everyone using
Bing, Copilot or ChatGPT search.

## Done when

The school appears in Bing Maps. Apple Maps only if Apple changes its rule (below).

## Notes (2026-11-05)

- Bing Places moved to bing.com/forbusiness in October 2025. "Import from Google" only verifies
  the Bing listing automatically when the Google profile is verified, and ours is not (d-018),
  so add it by hand and verify with a PIN (phone call or email; post is slow). Set the website
  to `https://seymadrivingschool.com/` (no www) and tick "Hide address".
- Apple Maps: Apple rejects businesses without premises customers can visit. Its rejection text
  reads "Maps only accepts businesses with a retail storefront or commercial location" that
  users can "visit during business hours without an appointment". Lessons start at the pupil's
  door, so the school does not qualify. Do not list a made-up or borrowed address to get round
  it. Look again if Apple adds service-area listings.

## Steps for Bing Places (owner, about 10 minutes)

1. Go to https://www.bing.com/forbusiness and sign in with a Microsoft account (the same one as
   Bing Webmaster Tools, if that was a Microsoft account).
2. Add the business by hand, not "Import from Google" (see the notes above).
3. Search for "Seyma Driving School". Claim it if it is already there; otherwise choose
   "my business is not listed", then "create new business".
4. Enter exactly what the website uses:
   - Name: Seyma Driving School
   - Phone: 07935 755151
   - Website: `https://seymadrivingschool.com/` (no www)
   - Category: Driving School
   - Address: the one on file with the Google profile, then under "Additional business details"
     tick "Hide address". Check the preview: Bing says it then shows only town and postcode, and
     a full UK postcode is close to a street, so stop and decide if it shows too much.
   - Areas served (if asked): Chingford, Loughton, Wanstead, Woodford, South Woodford,
     Buckhurst Hill, Walthamstow, Highams Park, Edmonton
   - Hours and description: same as Google, with "in English or Turkish" added
   - Email: info@seymadrivingschool.com
   - Photos: the car, and Seyma with the car if she is happy to be shown
5. Verify by PIN: email to info@seymadrivingschool.com if offered, otherwise a phone call when
   Seyma is not teaching. Avoid post (slow).
6. Afterwards: record the result here, mark this done, and add the Bing Maps link to the
   site's `sameAs` in the JSON-LD (EN and TR home pages).
