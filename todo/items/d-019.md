# d-019: Bing Places and Apple Business Connect

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

- Bing Places: add the listing (bing.com/forbusiness), verify it by PIN, hide the address.
- Apple Business Connect: claim or add the business, so it shows in Apple Maps and Siri.

Use the same name, phone, website and areas as the Google profile.

## Why

They cover the people who don't search on Google: iPhone users in Apple Maps, and everyone using
Bing, Copilot or ChatGPT search.

## Done when

The school appears in Bing Maps. Apple Maps only if Apple changes its rule (below).

## Notes (2026-11-05)

- Bing Places moved to bing.com/forbusiness in October 2025. "Import from Google" only verifies
  the Bing listing automatically when the Google profile is verified, and ours is not (d-018),
  so add it by hand and verify with a PIN (phone call or email; post is slow). Set the website
  to `https://seymadrivingschool.com/` (no www) and tick "Hide address".
- Apple Maps: Apple rejects businesses without premises customers can visit. Its rejection text
  reads "Maps only accepts businesses with a retail storefront or commercial location" that
  users can "visit during business hours without an appointment". Lessons start at the pupil's
  door, so the school does not qualify. Do not list a made-up or borrowed address to get round
  it. Look again if Apple adds service-area listings.
