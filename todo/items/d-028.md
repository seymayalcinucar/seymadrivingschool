# d-028: Review page with QR code and printable cards

- Status: done
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-05

## What

`review.html` and `tr/review.html`: a QR code that opens the Google review form, for pupils to
scan from Seyma's phone, plus a "Write a Google review" button for visitors on their own phone.
Printing the page gives an A4 sheet of 10 business-card-sized cards (85 x 55 mm) with cut lines.

## Notes

- The QR code is an inline SVG made with segno (error correction Q, 45 x 45 modules) from
  `https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid=ChIJ1c0cnw6DhyMR4hiwYBU74RI`. If the
  review link ever changes (see d-018), regenerate the `qr-review` symbol in both pages and
  reprint the cards.
- The pages are `noindex` and not in the sitemap: they are a tool for Seyma, not a search page.
  The footers keep a Google review link.
- Asking for reviews: ask every pupil, never offer anything in return, and never ask only the
  happy ones (Google's rules).
- Checked 2026-11-05: the QR decodes to the review link on screen (phone and desktop) and on all
  10 printed cards.
- 2026-11-06: at the owner's request, the footer link on every page now opens the business page in
  Google Maps (`https://maps.google.com/?cid=1360433525445105890`, rating, Reviews tab and "Write a
  review") in a new tab, not the write-a-review form. Only this page's QR code and button still go
  straight to the form, since pupils use them from Seyma's phone.
