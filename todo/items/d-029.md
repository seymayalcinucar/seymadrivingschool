# d-029: "How did you find Seyma?" on the enquiry form

- Status: done
- Priority: P1
- Added: 2026-11-06

## What

An optional dropdown at the end of the enquiry form on both home pages. The answer is added as the
last line of the WhatsApp message ("Found you through: Google Maps" in English, "Nereden duydum:
Google Haritalar" in Turkish) and left out when it is not answered. The plain WhatsApp links used
by browsers without `<dialog>` carry the same line as a blank.

Choices: Google search, Google Maps, a recommendation, social media, Nextdoor, GOV.UK instructor
search, a flyer, card or her car, somewhere else.

## Why

Analytics only counts visitors who accept cookies, so it cannot say which channel brings pupils.
The answers show which channels work (Google profile, directories, Nextdoor, groups, car signs,
any ads) so the ones that don't can be dropped.

## Notes

- The answer goes only into the WhatsApp message, never to analytics, so the privacy pages' promise
  holds. Both privacy pages now list it among what Seyma receives (updated 6 November 2026).
- How to use it: once a month, count the "Found you through" lines in new enquiries. A tally in
  phone notes is enough; WhatsApp Business labels per channel also work.
- If a new channel starts (Instagram, ads, a referral scheme), add it to the list in both
  `index.html` and `tr/index.html`, keeping the `value`s the same in both languages.
- Checked 2026-11-06 in Chromium at 320px, 390px and 1280px, English and Turkish: the message
  text, the line left out when unanswered, no sideways scrolling, no console errors.
