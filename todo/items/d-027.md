# d-027: Local groups, the Turkish community and social posts

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P3
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

- Local Facebook groups and Nextdoor for the areas covered, where parents ask for instructor
  recommendations. Check each group's rules on posting about a business.
- Turkish community: Facebook groups for Turkish speakers in London, London Turkish newspapers,
  and flyers with a QR code in Turkish shops in the areas covered.
- Instagram or TikTok: pass photos (with the pupil's agreement) and short tips, some in Turkish.
  The links go in the footer under d-012.

## Why

Word of mouth is how most learners pick an instructor.

## Done when

A routine is in place.

## Nextdoor routine (agreed 2026-11-09, business page approved that day)

- **How often:** weekly while the page is new (to the end of November), then every two weeks, Nextdoor's
  own advice (a post keeps circulating for about two weeks). The same text goes on her Google profile every
  week. Early evening, when people are home. The schedule is in d-032.
- **Reply to every comment** on the same day.
- **Recommendations matter most:** when someone in the area asks for an instructor, a happy pupil or parent
  recommending her does more than any post. Ask pupils who pass (and their parents) to recommend her on the
  page: footer link "Recommend Seyma on Nextdoor".
- **Rotation (one item per post):**
  1. A helpful tip: dark evenings (Nov), icy mornings (Dec to Feb), test-day checklist, "show me, tell me"
     (https://seymadrivingschool.com/show-me-tell-me.html).
  2. A pupil who passed: photo with the certificate and the test centre named, only with the pupil's OK
     (a parent's too if under 18).
  3. Local know-how: test centre pass rates (https://seymadrivingschool.com/test-centres.html) or the real
     cost of learning (https://seymadrivingschool.com/cost-of-learning-to-drive.html).
  4. About the lessons: a free slot if there is one, English or Turkish, the bring-a-friend offer once d-031
     is live.
- **Rules:** facts only from the site; never "for women" or "men and women" (memory rule, keep it neutral);
  WhatsApp first; her own photos, never stock; no pupil names or faces without permission.

### Post 1 (week of 9 Nov 2026): dark evenings

Photo: her car at dusk with the lights on.

> Darker evenings are here, so here are five tips for new and learner drivers:
>
> 1. Put your headlights on as it starts to get dark, not once it's fully dark.
> 2. Keep the windscreen clean inside and out. Smears make the glare from other lights much worse.
> 3. If oncoming headlights dazzle you, slow down, and stop if you need to.
> 4. Leave a bigger gap to the car in front. Speed and distance are harder to judge at night.
> 5. Look out for cyclists and people in dark clothes, especially near schools and bus stops.
