# d-027: Local groups, the Turkish community and social posts

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P3
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

- Local Facebook groups and Nextdoor for the areas covered, where parents ask for instructor
  recommendations. Check each group's rules on posting about a business.
- Turkish community: Facebook groups for Turkish speakers in London, London Turkish newspapers,
  and flyers with a QR code in Turkish shops in the areas covered.
- Instagram or TikTok: pass photos (with the pupil's agreement) and short tips, some in Turkish.
  The links go in the footer under d-012.

## Why

Word of mouth is how most learners pick an instructor.

## Done when

A routine is in place.
