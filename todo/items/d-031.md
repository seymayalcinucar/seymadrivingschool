# d-031: Recommend-a-friend reward

- Status: done on the site 2026-11-13 (printable "give this to a friend" cards not made yet)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-06

## What

A reward when a pupil recommends a friend who becomes a pupil.

## Agreed (2026-11-06)

- The friend: their first two-hour lesson is half price, £40 instead of £80. After that, the
  normal price.
- The pupil who recommended them: £40 off their next lesson, once the friend has had that first
  lesson.
- One discount per lesson. Two friends means £40 off two separate lessons, never one free lesson;
  each referral is used once.

## Decided 2026-11-13 (owner)

- "scheme is for current pupils only. for the time being there is no end date."

## Was still to decide

1. Past pupils who have passed have no next lesson: a voucher or cash instead, or the scheme is
   for current pupils only.
2. Any end date.
