# d-002: Google Search Console

- Status: in progress
- Priority: P1
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

Add `seymadrivingschool.com` as a property in Google Search Console, verify it, and submit `https://seymadrivingschool.com/sitemap.xml`.

## Why

It shows which Google searches bring people to the site and whether the pages are indexed. It sets no cookies, so it needs no consent banner.

## Done when

The sitemap shows as read in Search Console and the three pages are indexed.

## Notes

- Verification needs the owner's Google login. If Google gives an HTML file to upload, put it in `site/` and keep it there: removing it unverifies the site.
- 2026-11-04: added as a Domain property (covers www and http too), verified by a TXT record on
  `@` at Namecheap. Keep that record: removing it unverifies the property. The domain also has an
  older `google-site-verification` TXT record with a different code; it was left in place.
- 2026-11-04: verified; `sitemap.xml` submitted.
