# d-023: A page for each test centre

- Status: open (needs owner facts)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

One page each, in English and Turkish, for Chingford, Loughton and Wanstead: the test centre,
its pass rate from DVSA's published statistics, the areas around it, and local details from Seyma
(the roads and junctions pupils find hard, what a lesson there involves).

## Why

Competitors rank in these places with one page per area.

## Done when

The six pages are live and in the sitemap.

## Notes

- Each page needs content of its own. Near-copies with only the place name changed can get the
  site penalised.

## Questions for Seyma (what makes each page worth ranking)

For each centre:
1. Which roundabouts, junctions or roads do pupils find hardest on test routes?
2. Typical roads the test goes on (main roads, 30/40/50 limits, dual carriageway?).
3. Anything particular about the centre: where you wait, parking, the car park manoeuvre.
4. A tip she gives every pupil before a test there.
5. Roughly how many of her pupils have tested there (only if she wants it on the page).
