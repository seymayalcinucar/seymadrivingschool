# d-033: Home page that persuades visitors to message or call

- Status: in progress (owner facts)
- Priority: P1
- Added: 2026-11-11

## What

The owner asked for the home page to be "a lot more appealing", so that anyone who lands on it at
least calls or messages Seyma.

## Done 2026-11-11 (English and Turkish home pages)

- Hero: the Google rating (5.0, 17 reviews, five stars) straight under the headline, linked to the
  Maps business page; "£40 an hour · Two-hour lessons · No sign-up fee" before the buttons; "ask
  anything first, with no commitment". On a 390px phone the WhatsApp and Call buttons are on the
  first screen.
- Cookie banner: a slim bar shown on the first scroll (or after six seconds), never over the hero.
  Buttons are now "Accept" and "Reject"; both privacy pages quote the new names.
- New order: Why Seyma, Price, How it works, Lessons, The car, Areas, Questions, Contact.
- Lessons rewritten around the learner's situation ("Never driven, or nervous about it?", "Failed
  before, or worried about the test?"), each with its lesson type as a label.
- "How it works" starts with "Message Seyma" (no commitment); the provisional licence moved to step 2.
- A WhatsApp prompt at the end of Why Seyma, How it works, Lessons and Areas.
- "What you will learn" and "Why automatic" became two questions in Questions (no content lost).
- Every "Find us on Google" link on every page now goes to the Maps business page (the 2026-11-06 rule).

## Waiting for Seyma (each one is a bigger win than any layout change)

1. A photo of her, ideally smiling beside the car, and 3 or 4 lines about her: how long she has
   been teaching and what she is like as a teacher (also d-008).
2. 3 to 5 short quotes from her Google reviews, shown as first name and initial, once those pupils
   agree (d-007 rule: never invented or paraphrased).
3. Pass photos ("Passed at Chingford"), each with the pupil's agreement.
4. A reason to message now: a first-lesson offer, the friend referral (d-031), or a true
   availability line such as "Taking new pupils for January".
5. A reply time we can promise, for example "Replies the same day", only if it is true.

## Notes

- Copy rules still apply: WhatsApp first; "female instructor" stays; never "for women" or "men and
  women"; every new claim confirmed by the owner first.
