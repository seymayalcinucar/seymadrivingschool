# d-015: Check the safety list against the car

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

Check that the car has everything the "The car" section says it has.

## Why

The list was taken from Toyota's UK equipment list for the Corolla Design grade (dated January
2021) and from Euro NCAP's 2019 test result, not from the car itself. A 2020 car should match, but
equipment can differ between model years.

## What the page claims

- Dual controls with a brake on the instructor's side
- Five stars from Euro NCAP in the 2019 tests, 95% adult occupant protection
- Automatic emergency braking (Toyota's Pre-Collision System)
- Lane keeping help (Lane Departure Alert and Lane Trace Assist)
- Road sign display (Road Sign Assist)
- Reversing camera, and front and rear parking sensors
- Also fitted: seven airbags, stability and traction control, hill-start assist, adaptive cruise
  control, automatic high beam, automatic headlights and wipers, tyre pressure warning, eCall

## Done when

Each item is confirmed, or removed from `site/index.html` and `site/tr/index.html`.

## Notes

- Euro NCAP now marks the 2019 rating as expired because of its age, so the page says the car
  "earned" five stars in the 2019 tests and does not present it as a current rating.
