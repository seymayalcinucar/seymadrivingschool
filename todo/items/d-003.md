# d-003: WhatsApp Business greeting message

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P1
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

In the WhatsApp Business app: Settings → Business tools → Greeting message. Switch it on and paste the agreed text, saying £40 per hour and two-hour lessons.

## Why

Seyma is usually teaching. An automatic first reply tells an enquirer what to send and that she will get back to them.

## Done when

A first message from a new number gets the greeting automatically.

## Notes

- The greeting is sent on a first message, or after 14 days without contact. It needs the WhatsApp Business app, not ordinary WhatsApp.
