# d-024: Turkish guide to getting a UK licence

- Status: open
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

A Turkish page on getting a UK driving licence: the provisional licence, the theory test, the
practical test, and driving in the UK on a Turkish licence.

## Why

Turkish speakers in London search for exactly this. A page that answers it brings in people who
will need lessons.

## Done when

The page is live, linked from the Turkish home page, and read by the owner.

## Notes

- Every rule comes from GOV.UK and is linked to it. Check the rules again before publishing.
