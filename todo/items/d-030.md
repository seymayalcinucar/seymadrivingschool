## What

`cost-of-learning-to-drive.html` and `tr/cost-of-learning-to-drive.html`: the DVLA/DVSA fees, the
lesson price with example totals, ways to keep the cost down, and the 2026 test booking rules.
Linked from the price section of both home pages, every footer, and the Turkish guide's test fee
paragraph; in the sitemap.

## Why

People who search for prices are close to booking, and competitors rarely put the whole cost in
one place.
