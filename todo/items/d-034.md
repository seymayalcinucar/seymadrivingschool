# d-034: Lesson guide page with a printable progress checklist

- Status: done (published 2026-11-12)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-12

## What

The owner asked for documents about the lessons: the topics, and what kind of learning there is.
They chose one printable web page in English and Turkish over PDF-only handouts.

## Built 2026-11-12

- `site/what-you-will-learn.html` and `site/tr/derslerde-neler-ogrenilir.html`:
  - how a two-hour lesson works (four steps);
  - 26 DVSA learner skills in five stages (first lessons; roads and junctions; busier roads and
    roundabouts; manoeuvres and the emergency stop; test ready), with "In the test" notes;
  - the driving test at a glance, the theory test and practice, and the five DVSA progress levels;
  - the Turkish page shows each skill's English name, because the test is only in English.
- Printed or saved as a PDF: four A4 pages of guide plus a one-page progress checklist (26 skills x
  5 levels), with the phone number and website at the top of each.
- Linked from both home pages (Lessons section and the "What will I learn?" answer), every footer
  and the sitemap. The other pages' menus now follow the home page order.

## How she teaches: approved 2026-11-12

The skills and test facts come from DVSA and GOV.UK. These lines describe how she teaches; the owner
reviewed the pages and approved them on 2026-11-12:

1. Step 2, "Today's goal": you look back at your last lesson and agree what to work on today.
2. Step 3, "Practice": plenty of time on roads that suit the skill, starting on quieter roads while
   it is new.
3. Step 4, "Look back": you talk through how it went and what comes next.
4. The five stages as the usual order of lessons (the page says they are a guide, not a timetable).
