# d-016: Price card is too wide on 320px phones

- Status: done
- Priority: P3
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

On a screen 320px wide the price card is about 15px wider than the page, so the page scrolls
sideways a little. The "Message on WhatsApp" button inside the card cannot shrink below its text.

## Why

Found while checking the header at phone widths. It was already there before the Turkish pages
were added. Screens this narrow are rare (the first iPhone SE); 360px and wider are fine.

## Done when

At 320px wide the page is no wider than the screen.

## Done (2026-11-05)

Two causes, not one. The footer was the bigger one: below 704px it has two columns and the email
address (about 230px) forced the left column wide, so pages were up to 359px wide at 320px.
"Get in touch" now takes the full row there and the columns use `minmax(0, 1fr)`. The price
card's "Message on WhatsApp" button (259px, no wrapping) now fits at 320px with less padding,
and may wrap as a fallback. Single-column `.hero-grid` and `.split` use `minmax(0, 1fr)` so
content can't widen the page. Every page checked at 320, 360 and 390px: no sideways scroll.
