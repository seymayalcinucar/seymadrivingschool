# d-020: GOV.UK "Find a driving instructor" listing

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

Opt in to the GOV.UK service for learners to find driving instructors
(gov.uk/find-driving-schools-and-lessons). ADIs choose whether to be listed: sign in to the ADI
online services with the ADI reference number and switch the listing on.

## Why

It is free, it is official, and learners who want to check that an instructor is approved use it.

## Done when

A search of the service by postcode (for example E4) shows Seyma.
