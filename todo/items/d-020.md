# d-020: GOV.UK "Find a driving instructor" listing

- Status: done
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-11-04

## What

Opt in to the GOV.UK service for learners to find driving instructors
(gov.uk/find-driving-schools-and-lessons). ADIs choose whether to be listed: sign in to the ADI
online services with the ADI reference number and switch the listing on.

## Why

It is free, it is official, and learners who want to check that an instructor is approved use it.

## Done when

A search of the service by postcode (for example E4) shows Seyma.

## Steps (checked on GOV.UK 2026-11-05)

Only Seyma can do this: it needs her DVSA online account.

1. Go to https://www.gov.uk/update-approved-driving-instructor-registration and press "Start now"
   (the service is open 6am to 11:30pm).
2. Sign in with her **DVSA online user ID** and password. The user ID is on letters from DVSA and
   is not the ADI number. If it is lost, request it with her driving licence number:
   https://submit.forms.service.gov.uk/form/2634/request-your-driving-instructor-services-user-id
   (help: customerservices@dvsa.gov.uk).
3. Choose to publish her details on "Find driving schools, lessons and instructors":
   - yes to publishing the main phone number and email: 07935 755151, info@seymadrivingschool.com
   - website: https://seymadrivingschool.com/
   - tick the boxes for keeping skills up to date (CPD) and following the ADI code of practice;
     learners can filter on both
   - publishing the standards check grade is optional: her choice
4. Changes show the next day. Check by searching E4 at https://www.gov.uk/find-driving-schools-and-lessons
