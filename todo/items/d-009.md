# d-009: Mark the enquiry event as a key event

- Status: open (owner)
- Priority: P3
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

In Google Analytics: Admin → Events, and mark `generate_lead` as a key event.

## Why

`generate_lead` is sent when a visitor who accepted analytics cookies sends the WhatsApp form. As a key event it is reported as a conversion.

## Notes

- `call_tap` is sent when a call link is tapped. The event only appears in the list after it has happened once.
