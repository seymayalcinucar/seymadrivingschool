# d-007: Reviews section

- Status: open
- Priority: P2
- Added: 2026-10-12

## What

Add a reviews section with real reviews, or show the Google rating, once the Google profile has some.

## Why

Reviews are what the comparable local sites lead with.

## Done when

The page shows genuine reviews with the reviewers' agreement.

## Notes

- Never invented or paraphrased reviews. Needs the review text, or the rating and count from the Google profile.
- 2026-11-04: the Google profile has 5.0 stars from 16 reviews. Show the rating and count with a
  link to the reviews (`https://search.google.com/local/reviews?placeid=ChIJ1c0cnw6DhyMR4hiwYBU74RI`)
  and a "Leave a review" link (see d-018). Update the count now and then. No `aggregateRating` in
  the JSON-LD: Google ignores a business's ratings of itself and can treat them as spam.
