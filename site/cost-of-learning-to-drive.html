Ways to keep the cost down

  • Apply for your provisional licence online: £34 instead of £43 by post.
  • Pass your theory test early. You need it before you can book your driving test, so it will not hold you up later.
  • Take your driving test on a weekday if you can: £62 instead of £75. A test from 4:30pm counts as an evening test.
  • Book a block of lessons for the discount.
  • Practise between lessons with family or friends if you can. Anyone who supervises you must be over 21, be qualified to drive the type of car, and have held a full licence for 3 years (from the UK, the EU, Switzerland, Norway, Iceland or Liechtenstein). You must also be insured to drive the car as a learner. The rules are on GOV.UK.
  • Book your test when you are nearly ready. You can only change it twice, and a retest costs the full fee again.

Booking your driving test: the 2026 rules

DVSA changed the rules for booking car driving tests in 2026:

  • Only you can book and manage your test (since 12 May 2026). Your instructor, or anyone else, cannot do it for you.
  • Services that scan the booking system for earlier tests are not allowed (since 12 May 2026), so there is no need to pay anyone to find you a date. Book on GOV.UK: new dates are released at 6am every Monday, 24 weeks ahead, and earlier dates can appear at any time when other people change their tests.
  • You can change your test only twice (since 31 March 2026). A change is free with 10 full working days’ notice.
  • You can only move your test to one of the 3 centres nearest the one you booked (since 9 June 2026), so book at a test centre you mean to use.
