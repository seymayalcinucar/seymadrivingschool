What you will learn

Everything the driving test asks for

Lessons build up step by step, from the controls of the car to driving on your own.

  • The car and moving off

    Getting comfortable with the controls and mirrors, then moving off and stopping safely.

  • Junctions and roundabouts

    Approaching, emerging and turning at junctions, crossroads and roundabouts.

  • Sharing the road

    Meeting other traffic, pedestrian crossings and spotting hazards early.

  • Faster roads

    Main roads and dual carriageways: joining, keeping pace and changing lanes.

  • Manoeuvres

    Parallel parking, parking in a bay and pulling up on the right, as used in the test.

  • Ready for the test

    Independent driving with a sat nav or road signs, the emergency stop and the “show me, tell me” questions.

Why automatic

Less to juggle, more time to learn

In an automatic the car looks after the gears. That leaves you free to concentrate on what is happening around you.

Good to know: if you pass your test in an automatic, your licence covers automatic cars only. You can take a manual test later if you want to drive manual cars as well.

  • Easier starts

    Pulling away at junctions, in queues and on hills is simpler, which takes a lot of pressure off the early lessons.

  • Calmer in traffic

    Stop-start traffic is far less tiring when you are not changing gear every few seconds.

  • Ready for electric

    Electric cars and most hybrids are automatic, so what you learn carries straight over to them.

Questions

What learners ask before they start

Something else on your mind? Message Seyma on WhatsApp.

Do I need a licence before my first lesson?

Yes. You need a provisional driving licence before you can drive on public roads. You can apply on GOV.UK once you are 15 years and 9 months old, and start driving a car when you are 17.

Can I start lessons before I pass my theory test?

Yes. You can take lessons while you study for it. You need to pass the theory test before you can book your practical test.

Is the driving test different in an automatic?

No. The practical test is the same. You simply take it in an automatic car.

If I pass in an automatic, can I drive a manual car?

Not on that licence. An automatic licence covers automatic cars only. If you want to drive manual cars later, you can take a manual test to upgrade.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone is different. It depends on your experience and on how much practice you get between lessons. Message Seyma to talk through where you are now.

How much do lessons cost?

£45 per hour. Lessons are two hours long, so £90 a lesson, with a discount if you book a block. Send a WhatsApp message for details.

How long is a lesson?

Two hours. That gives you time to settle in, practise properly and finish on something you have got right.

Can I use Seyma's car for my test?

Yes. On test day she picks you up, takes you for a warm-up drive, you sit the test in her car, and she drops you home afterwards.

Will Seyma pick me up?

Yes. She collects you from home, work, school or college and drops you back at the end of the lesson.

Can I have my lessons in Turkish?

Yes. Seyma teaches in English or Turkish. Dersler Türkçe de yapılır.

Which test centres do you cover?

Chingford, Loughton and Wanstead. Lessons also cover Woodford, South Woodford, Buckhurst Hill, Chigwell, Walthamstow and Highams Park.
