A five-star car to learn in

Lessons are in a Toyota Corolla automatic with dual controls. It earned the top five-star rating in Euro NCAP's safety tests, and it has systems that watch the road with you.

  • Dual controls

    Seyma has her own brake on the passenger side, so she can step in at any moment.

  • Five stars from Euro NCAP

    The Corolla earned the top rating in Euro NCAP's 2019 crash tests, with 95% for protecting adults in the car.

  • Automatic emergency braking

    Watches the road ahead for vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists. It warns the driver and can brake by itself if a collision is close.

    Warns you if the car drifts out of its lane without indicating, and can steer it gently back.

Questions

What learners ask before they start

Something else on your mind? Message Seyma on WhatsApp.

Do I need a licence before my first lesson?

Yes. You need a provisional driving licence before you can drive on public roads. You can apply on GOV.UK once you are 15 years and 9 months old, and start driving a car when you are 17.

Can I start lessons before I pass my theory test?

Yes. You can take lessons while you study for it. You need to pass the theory test before you can book your practical test.

Is the driving test different in an automatic?

No. The practical test is the same. You simply take it in an automatic car.

Why learn in an automatic?

The car looks after the gears, so you can concentrate on what is happening around you. Pulling away at junctions, in queues and on hills is simpler, which takes a lot of pressure off the early lessons, and stop-start traffic is far less tiring. Electric cars and most hybrids are automatic too, so what you learn carries straight over to them.

If I pass in an automatic, can I drive a manual car?

Not on that licence. An automatic licence covers automatic cars only. If you want to drive manual cars later, you can take a manual test to upgrade.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone is different. It depends on your experience and on how much practice you get between lessons. Message Seyma to talk through where you are now.

What will I learn?

Everything the driving test asks for, step by step: the controls, mirrors, moving off and stopping; junctions, crossroads and roundabouts; meeting other traffic, pedestrian crossings and spotting hazards early; main roads and dual carriageways; the manoeuvres used in the test (parallel parking, parking in a bay and pulling up on the right); and independent driving with a sat nav or road signs, the emergency stop and the “show me, tell me” questions. See every skill, stage by stage.
