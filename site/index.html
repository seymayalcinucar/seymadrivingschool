Why learn in an automatic

No clutch, no gears

With no clutch to balance and no gears to change, you can keep your attention on the road and on other road users.

Easier starts

Pulling away at junctions, in queues and on hills is simpler, which takes a lot of pressure off the early lessons.

Ready for electric

Electric cars and most hybrids are automatic, so what you learn carries straight over to them.

Good to know: if you pass your test in an automatic, your licence covers automatic cars only. You can take a manual test later if you want to drive manual cars as well.
