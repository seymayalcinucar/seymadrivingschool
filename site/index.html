Why automatic

Less to juggle, more time to learn

In an automatic the car looks after the gears. That leaves you free to concentrate on what is happening around you.

Good to know: if you pass your test in an automatic, your licence covers automatic cars only. You can take a manual test later if you want to drive manual cars as well.

  • Easier starts

    Pulling away at junctions, in queues and on hills is simpler, which takes a lot of pressure off the early lessons.

  • Calmer in traffic

    Stop-start traffic is far less tiring when you are not changing gear every few seconds.

  • Ready for electric

    Electric cars and most hybrids are automatic, so what you learn carries straight over to them.

Questions

What learners ask before they start

Something else on your mind? Call Seyma on 07935 755151.

Do I need a licence before my first lesson?

Yes. You need a provisional driving licence before you can drive on public roads. You can apply on GOV.UK once you are 15 years and 9 months old, and start driving a car when you are 17.

Can I start lessons before I pass my theory test?

Yes. You can take lessons while you study for it. You need to pass the theory test before you can book your practical test.

Is the driving test different in an automatic?

No. The practical test is the same. You simply take it in an automatic car.

If I pass in an automatic, can I drive a manual car?

Not on that licence. An automatic licence covers automatic cars only. If you want to drive manual cars later, you can take a manual test to upgrade.

How many lessons will I need?

Everyone is different. It depends on your experience and on how much practice you get between lessons. Call to talk through where you are now.
