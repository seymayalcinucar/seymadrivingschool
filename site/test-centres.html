What the figures show

Only Enfield (53.9%) is above the Great Britain average of 50.0%. Barnet is level with it at 49.9%, and the other six centres are below it.

Chingford has the lowest pass rate of the 8 (39.9%), up from 36.5% the year before.

Nationally, automatic tests have a lower pass rate than car tests overall: 44.6% against 50.0%. The same is true at all 8 centres.

A pass rate also depends on who tests at each centre, so treat it as a rough guide. Choose your test centre with care: since 9 June 2026 you can only move a test to one of the 3 centres nearest the one you booked. It helps to have lessons on the roads around the centre you choose.
